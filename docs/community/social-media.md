# SharePoint developer social media and update channels

Use these channels to follow SharePoint and Microsoft 365 development. Start with the [Microsoft 365 & Power Platform Community](https://pnp.github.io/) for learning resources and ways to participate.

## Blogs, videos, and community calls

- [Microsoft 365 Developer Blog](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/microsoft365dev/) - Developer news and technical articles.
- [Microsoft Community Learning on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MicrosoftCommunityLearning/videos) - Tutorials, demos, and call recordings.
- [Community videos](https://aka.ms/community/videos) - Browse learning resources from the community site.
- [Community calls](https://aka.ms/community/calls) - Current schedules, invitations, and joining details.

## LinkedIn

- [Microsoft 365 Developer](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/microsoft365dev/)
- [Microsoft 365 & Power Platform Community](https://www.linkedin.com/company/m365pnp/)

## X

- [@Microsoft365Dev](https://x.com/Microsoft365Dev)

- [@m365pnp](https://x.com/m365pnp)

## Questions and feedback

Social channels aren't a substitute for an issue report or a support request. See [Support and feedback](../support-feedback.md) for product bugs, documentation feedback, how-to questions, and library or sample defects.
